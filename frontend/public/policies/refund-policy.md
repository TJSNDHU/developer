## 1.5 Situations where a refund WILL be considered

- A billing error caused by AUREM (e.g. duplicate charge, incorrect plan charged).
- Extended platform outage on our side that materially prevented you from using the service for `[X consecutive hours/days]` during your billing period.

- Dissatisfaction with AI-generated code quality or output (the nature of AI generation means output quality varies; we do not guarantee fitness for any particular purpose).

- Suspension or termination of your account for a violation of the Acceptable Use Policy — no refund is owed in these cases.
- Third-party service costs already incurred on your behalf (e.g. compute already provisioned for a dedicated database).

- You may request a full data export before the grace period ends by `[describe process/contact]`.
- We are not responsible for data loss if no action is taken within the grace period stated at the time of downgrade.

## 1.8 Cancellation

You may cancel auto-renewal at any time from **Account Settings → Billing**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period — you retain access until then, with no partial-period refund for the unused portion, except where Section 1.5 applies.

## 1.9 Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank/card issuer instead of contacting us first, we reserve the right to immediately suspend your account and any deployed applications while the dispute is investigated.
