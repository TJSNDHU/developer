# Acceptable Use Policy — AUREM CTO Dev
**Last updated: February 7, 2026**

This policy defines what you may and may not do with AUREM CTO Dev.

---

## Permitted Uses

- Writing, fixing, and refactoring code for legitimate software projects
- Security auditing your own codebases
- Building personal and commercial software products
- Automating legitimate development workflows
- Learning and educational purposes

---

## Prohibited Uses

### Malicious Code
You may not use the Service to generate:
- Malware, ransomware, spyware, or viruses
- Code designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to systems
- Exploits, rootkits, or keyloggers
- Phishing pages or fraudulent interfaces

### Illegal Activity
You may not use the Service to:
- Violate any applicable local, national, or international law
- Generate code that facilitates illegal activity
- Infringe intellectual property rights
- Process personal data without legal basis

### Abuse
You may not:
- Attempt to circumvent rate limits or usage caps
- Use automated scripts to abuse the Service
- Resell or sublicense access without permission
- Attempt to reverse engineer or copy the Service
- Use the Service to compete with us without prior agreement

### Harmful Content
You may not generate code or content that:
- Discriminates based on protected characteristics
- Facilitates harassment or abuse
- Violates children's safety

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Warning and task rejection
- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent account termination without refund
- Reporting to law enforcement where required

---
